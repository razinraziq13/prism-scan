Tips

  • Install on iPhone: in Safari tap ••• or Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Install on Android: in Chrome tap ⋮, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  • Sign: open a page and tap Sign. Your saved signatures stay on this phone.
  • Best scans: put the paper on a dark, plain surface with good light.
  • Corners: drag the colored dots. A zoom bubble helps you place them exactly.
  • Magic filter whitens paper and sharpens text. B&W is best for printing.
  • Text (OCR) needs internet the first time each language is used.
  • Private: scans stay on this phone. Export important PDFs to Files as a backup.
